Sizesof Runeterra

In the game every champion is roughly the same size so the fight stays fair. In the lore they're not. Here they stand side by side at their real, stated heights.

Only heights Riot or an official source has actually stated are drawn here; each one shows where it comes from. More champions join the plate as Riot reveals their sizes.
