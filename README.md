# Lore Explorer

An interactive fan atlas of the League of Legends universe: a painted map of Runeterra, every region in its own visual language, all 173 champions, and the bonds between them.

**Live site:** https://devdeepsinghbhuee.github.io/lore-explorer/

## What's inside
- Interactive, zoomable map of Runeterra with region highlights and lore locations
- 14 regions (13 nations plus the Wanderers), each themed after its magic, materials and architecture
- Official region paintings from Legends of Runeterra with depth-based parallax (depth maps made with Depth Anything V2)
- All 173 champions with official art, roles, bios, 400 relationships, official stories, cards and videos
- Library: all 1,647 Legends of Runeterra cards with their lore (library.html)
- Riftbound: every card of League's trading card game from Riot's official gallery (riftbound.html)
- Theater: 147 official cinematics, Worlds anthems, Arcane trailers and music videos (theater.html)

## Project layout
- `index.html` the whole site (no build step)
- `data/champions.js` champion data
- `img/` champion art, region emblems and paintings
